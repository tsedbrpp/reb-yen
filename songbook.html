About the Songwriter

Reb Yen

Singer-Songwriter & Multi-Instrumentalist · Boulder, CO & Orlando, FL

Reb Yen writes, arranges, and performs songs that move between autobiographical reflection and world observations. Based in Boulder, Colorado, and Orlando, Florida, fans and critics describe Reb as a strong vocalist and accomplished musician, playing guitar, keyboard, and harmonica across Americana, Folk, Country, Pop, Rock, and Indie.

Reb has toured internationally, including two tours of Europe, and spent time immersed in Nashville’s songwriting community. Her performance credits include Walt Disney World, Universal Studios, and New York City’s Lincoln Center, where she performed as a featured artist with the Aman World Music & Dance Ensemble (Los Angeles, CA), as well as the Commodore Grille in Nashville.

She received formal training in singing and vocal technique and has experience as a vocal coach, including professional involvement with the National Association of Teachers of Singing (NATS).
