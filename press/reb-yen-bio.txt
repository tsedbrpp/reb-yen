REB YEN — SHORT BIOGRAPHY

Reb Yen is a Boulder-based singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist with roots in Orlando, Florida. Her original songs span Americana, folk, indie, country, pop and rock. Reb writes and arranges her own music and performs on guitar, banjo, keyboard and harmonica. Her live performances combine original songs and carefully chosen covers, in solo, duo or full-band formats.

CAREER BACKGROUND

Reb began her professional career as a singer and dancer with the Aman World Music & Dance Ensemble in Los Angeles, including a performance at Lincoln Center. She later worked as a singer, dancer and producer at Walt Disney World and toured internationally with original performance art combining music, dance and illusion. Her career also includes Nashville songwriting and performances across Colorado.
