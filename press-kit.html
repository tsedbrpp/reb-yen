Short biography

Reb Yen writes, arranges and performs songs that move between autobiographical reflection and world observations. Based in Boulder, Colorado, and Orlando, Florida, fans and critics describe Reb as a strong vocalist and accomplished musician, playing guitar, keyboard, and harmonica. She performs multiple genres, including Americana, Folk, Country, Pop, Rock, and Indie. Reb has toured internationally, including two tours of Europe, and spent time immersed in Nashville’s songwriting community.

Career & performance background

Yen’s performance credits include Walt Disney World, Universal Studios, and New York City’s Lincoln Center, where she performed as a featured artist with the Aman World Music & Dance Ensemble (Los Angeles, CA). She has also performed at the Commodore Grille in Nashville, a venue known for showcasing emerging and established songwriters.

Reb received formal training in singing and vocal technique and has experience as a vocal coach, including professional involvement with the National Association of Teachers of Singing (NATS).

What the critics are saying

“Most love songs either put romance on a pedestal or get lost in the heartbreak. On Take Me Crazy, Reb Yen does neither. She writes about real people with real flaws, and she has that rare Nashville knack for dropping you right into the middle of a conversation. One line makes you smile and the very next line hits home, delivered with the easy confidence of a singer who knows how to hold a room.”

Performance formats

Solo acoustic, duo, or full band. Original songs and carefully chosen covers for venues, private events, and festivals.
