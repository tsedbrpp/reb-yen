Short biography

Reb Yen is a singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist based in Boulder, Colorado, with roots in Orlando, Florida. Her original music spans Americana, folk, indie, country, pop, and rock. Playing guitar, banjo, keyboard, and harmonica, she brings her songs to the stage as a solo performer, in a duo, or with a full band. Her live performances include originals and carefully chosen covers. Hear her original recordings on Bandcamp and watch her live performances on YouTube.

Performance formats

Solo acoustic, duo, or full band. Original songs and carefully chosen covers for venues, private events, and festivals.
