Biography

Fans and critics describe Reb Yen as a strong vocalist and accomplished musician whose songwriting bridges autobiographical storytelling and observations on everyday life. Based in Boulder, Colorado, and Orlando, Florida, she performs on guitar, keyboard, and harmonica across Americana, Folk, Country, Pop, Rock, and Indie. Over the course of her career, Reb has toured internationally, including two tours of Europe, and spent formative time immersed in Nashville’s songwriting community.

Stage & touring credits

Reb began her professional performance career in Los Angeles with the acclaimed Aman World Music & Dance Ensemble, appearing as a featured artist at New York City’s Lincoln Center. Her major stage credits also include extended performance work at Walt Disney World and Universal Studios in Orlando, as well as songwriter showcases at Nashville’s historic Commodore Grille and Front Range venues in Boulder, Colorado.

Vocal training & coaching

Alongside her recording and touring work, Reb received formal training in singing and vocal technique. She has worked as a vocal coach helping singers develop healthy technique and stage confidence, including professional involvement with the National Association of Teachers of Singing (NATS).
