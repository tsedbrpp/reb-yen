Short biography

Reb Yen is a singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist based in Boulder, Colorado, and Orlando, Florida. Her original Americana, folk, indie, country, pop and rock songs range from personal reflections to observations of the wider world. She writes and arranges her music and performs on guitar, banjo, keyboard and harmonica. Her live sets mix original songs with carefully chosen covers and are available as solo, duo or full-band performances.

Career & performance background

Reb began her professional performance career as a singer and dancer with the Aman World Music & Dance Ensemble in Los Angeles, including an appearance at Lincoln Center in New York. Her work later took her to Walt Disney World in Florida, where she worked as a singer, dancer and producer. She also created and toured an original performance-art act that brought music, dance and illusion together, with performances across North America and Europe. Her musical journey includes time in Nashville’s songwriting community and current performances in Colorado.

Artist recognition

Reb’s ReverbNation artist profile reports that Take Me Crazy, Should I?, and Gotta Let It Go earned Featured Artist placement on the ReverbNation homepage. Listen to her original recordings on Bandcamp.

Performance formats

Solo acoustic, duo, or full band. Original songs and carefully chosen covers for venues, private events, and festivals.
